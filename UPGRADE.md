# Upgrade

Upgrade from any previous master release version of ClicShopping and CLicShoppingAi. The upgrade procedure will sort out and apply all
necessary version patches.

Note: Add-ons are version specific and might cause your upgraded platform to malfunction. Make sure all your add-ons are
up to date.

### Instructions

1. Backup your files AND database!! Do not underestimate the damage that can be caused by a failed upgrade process.

2. **Upload the contents/** to the corresponding path of your installation **replacing the current files**. Any modified
   files will be overwritten!

   If you have renamed your admin/ folder, make sure the files of the admin/ folder are uploaded to the right place.

3.read the directory sql_update and follow the instructions

4. Make sure everything went fine

If you need help, turn to our forums at https://www.clicshopping.org/forums/.
