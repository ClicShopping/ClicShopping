**Commercial Use Threshold --- Practical Notice**

US 500,000 (worldwide, all affiliated entities combined) are required

-   AGPLv3 source-disclosure obligations (including for SaaS and network

-   A commercial license simplifies compliance, removes source-disclosure
    obligations, and provides a contractual framework suited to
