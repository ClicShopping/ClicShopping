text_info_order_total_sequence = The order total modules are calculated in a SEQUENCE, and that sequence has a legal meaning: the base (sub-total), then the reductions granted on the sale, then the charges that enter the taxable base (shipping, handling), then the tax, then the charges outside the taxable base, then the grand total, which is always last. The platform places each module at its rank on its own: a module declares its fiscal role, and a module that declares none is refused at installation with a message saying so.<br />Two orders coexist and they are NOT the same: the CALCULATION follows the ranks above, while the Sort order below only drives the order in which the lines are PRINTED on the invoice. Editing it stays yours to do and changes nothing in the calculation.<br />A chain installed by an earlier version keeps the order it was given then. If an invoice shows a reduction after the tax, or a line the grand total does not contain, uninstall that module and install it again: it will be placed at its rank. Uninstalling also clears that module's own settings, so note them first.
