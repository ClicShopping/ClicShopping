# AI Act Compliance Statement

**Project:** ClicShopping AI
**Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-06

---

## Purpose

This document describes the measures implemented by ClicShopping AI to support compliance with Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act) 
when AI-assisted features are used within the platform.

ClicShopping AI is an open source application providing AI-assisted tools for e-commerce management. 
It is not an AI model and does not train foundation models. It acts as an interface between the user and one or more external or local AI providers.

---

# Scope

This document applies to AI-assisted features available within ClicShopping AI, including but not limited to:

* Product description generation
* Product content enhancement
* FAQ generation
* SEO content generation
* Report generation
* Product creation assistance
* Administrative writing assistance

The availability of individual features depends on the installed applications and configuration.

---

# AI Provider

ClicShopping AI does not develop, train or modify artificial intelligence models.

The software connects to AI services configured by the administrator.

Supported providers may include:

* OpenAI
* Anthropic
* Google Gemini
* Mistral AI
* Ollama
* Other compatible providers

The administrator is responsible for selecting the AI provider appropriate for their legal, privacy and security requirements.

---

# Human Oversight

All generated content is intended to assist users.

Generated content should be reviewed before publication or use.

ClicShopping AI provides content suggestions and does not guarantee:

* factual accuracy;
* legal compliance;
* commercial suitability;
* SEO performance;
* absence of hallucinations.

The final decision remains under the responsibility of the user.

---

# Transparency

Users interact intentionally with AI-assisted features.

AI functionality is accessed through dedicated interfaces and actions initiated by the user.

Generated content should be identified within the interface as AI-generated whenever applicable.

---

# User Responsibility

The user remains responsible for:

* reviewing generated content;
* validating information;
* correcting inaccuracies;
* ensuring legal compliance;
* ensuring compliance with copyright laws;
* ensuring compliance with consumer protection laws;
* ensuring compliance with applicable regulations.

Publication of generated content is the responsibility of the user.

---

# Data Processing

Depending on the configured AI provider, prompts and related content may be transmitted to external services.

The transmitted data depends on:

* the selected AI provider;
* the configuration chosen by the administrator;
* the generated request.

Administrators are responsible for informing users of applicable privacy policies and ensuring compliance with data protection legislation, including the GDPR where applicable.

Sensitive or confidential information should not be transmitted unless appropriate safeguards have been implemented.

---

# Logging

ClicShopping AI may record technical information related to AI requests for administration, auditing or troubleshooting purposes.

Examples include:

* request date and time;
* AI provider;
* model identifier;
* requesting user;
* feature used.

The storage and retention of logs depend on the system configuration.

---

# Limitations

AI-generated content may contain:

* factual errors;
* outdated information;
* incomplete information;
* incorrect references;
* formatting inconsistencies.

Generated content should not be considered authoritative without verification.

---

# High-Risk AI Systems

Based on its intended purpose, ClicShopping AI is designed as an AI-assisted content generation platform.

It is not intended to perform activities listed as high-risk AI systems under Annex III of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, including but not limited to:

* biometric identification;
* recruitment or employment decisions;
* education assessment;
* creditworthiness assessment;
* law enforcement;
* migration management;
* judicial decision support;
* access to essential public or private services.

If ClicShopping AI is extended or integrated into such use cases, additional legal obligations may apply.
