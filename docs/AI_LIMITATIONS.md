# AI_LIMITATIONS.md

# AI Limitations

## Purpose

This document describes the known limitations of AI-assisted features available in ClicShopping AI.

Artificial intelligence generates content based on statistical inference and does not possess knowledge, reasoning, or understanding comparable to human expertise.

Users remain responsible for reviewing all generated content before use or publication.

---

# General Limitations

AI-generated content may:

* contain factual errors;
* contain outdated information;
* omit important details;
* generate inaccurate statements;
* produce inconsistent answers;
* misunderstand ambiguous prompts;
* generate repetitive content;
* produce unexpected or irrelevant results.

The quality of the output depends on the quality and clarity of the input provided.

---

# No Guarantee of Accuracy

ClicShopping AI does not guarantee that generated content is:

* factually correct;
* complete;
* current;
* reliable;
* free of errors.

Generated content should always be verified using trusted sources when accuracy is important.

---

# No Legal Advice

AI-generated content must not be considered:

* legal advice;
* regulatory guidance;
* tax advice;
* financial advice;
* medical advice.

Professional consultation may be required depending on the intended use.

---

# No Business Decision Support

AI-generated content should not be the sole basis for:

* commercial decisions;
* pricing strategies;
* contractual obligations;
* compliance assessments;
* risk management decisions.

Human review remains essential.

---

# SEO Limitations

SEO-related content generated by AI does not guarantee:

* improved search engine rankings;
* increased website traffic;
* better conversion rates;
* compliance with search engine guidelines.

Search engine algorithms evolve continuously and remain outside the control of ClicShopping AI.

---

# Product Information

When generating or enhancing product content, AI may:

* invent specifications;
* generate incorrect dimensions or weights;
* confuse similar products;
* create unsupported technical claims;
* generate inaccurate compatibility information.

Official manufacturer documentation should be used to verify technical information.

---

# Translation Limitations

AI-generated translations may:

* lose contextual meaning;
* mistranslate technical terminology;
* introduce grammatical errors;
* produce culturally inappropriate wording.

Translations should be reviewed before publication.

---

# Hallucinations

AI models may generate information that appears plausible but is incorrect or unsupported.

Examples include:

* non-existent product features;
* incorrect references;
* fabricated standards or regulations;
* fictitious company names;
* invented technical specifications.

Users should independently verify important information.

---

# External Knowledge

AI providers have knowledge limitations that may include:

* outdated training data;
* incomplete domain expertise;
* lack of awareness of recent events;
* absence of proprietary or private business information unless explicitly provided.

Generated responses reflect the capabilities of the selected AI provider.

---

# Provider Differences

Results may vary depending on:

* the selected AI provider;
* the AI model;
* model version;
* prompt formulation;
* provider configuration.

The same prompt may produce different outputs across providers or over time.

---

# Availability

AI functionality depends on external or local services.

Service availability may be affected by:

* provider outages;
* API limits;
* rate limiting;
* network connectivity;
* maintenance operations;
* local hardware resources for self-hosted models.

ClicShopping AI cannot guarantee uninterrupted availability.

---

# Privacy Considerations

When external AI providers are used, submitted information may leave the local environment.

Users should avoid submitting confidential, personal, or sensitive information unless such use complies with their organization's policies and applicable laws.

---

# Human Review

Human review is recommended before:

* publishing content;
* distributing generated documents;
* modifying product information;
* generating customer-facing material;
* making business decisions.

AI should be considered an assistance tool rather than an authoritative source.

---

# Future Improvements

AI technologies evolve continuously.

Future versions of ClicShopping AI may improve capabilities, add new providers, or introduce additional safeguards, but no guarantee is made regarding the behavior or performance of third-party AI models.

---

# Disclaimer

ClicShopping AI provides access to AI-assisted features but does not control the behavior of external AI models.

The quality, accuracy, and reliability of generated content depend on the selected AI provider and the input supplied by the user.

The user remains solely responsible for reviewing, validating, and approving all AI-generated content before use.
