"clarify" is reserved for EXACTLY TWO cases: this missing-period rule, and temporal_unit_missing below. Every other kind of ambiguity — scope, metric, quantification — is answered with the most reasonable default ("use_default" or "generate_both") and NEVER stops to ask. In particular a plain request to LIST records of a state or a category is answered, never clarified: it has an obvious reading, and asking "all or only the active ones?" helps nobody.
This rule takes PRECEDENCE over the quantitative-language, revenue, multi-field and group-by rules below. Those rules settle WHICH metric to compute and WHICH dimension to group by; they never settle WHICH PERIOD to cover. A query can be perfectly explicit about its metric and still be missing its period — and then it is ambiguous.
