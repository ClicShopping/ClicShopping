text_metric_catalog_intro = KNOWN METRICS. Use ONLY these names in the plan. Each line gives the metric name, the GRAIN it lives at, its TYPE and what it means. A metric absent from this list does not exist: do not invent one.

text_metric_catalog_row = - {{name}} | grain: {{grain}} | type: {{type}} | {{definition}}

text_metric_revenue_ttc = INVOICED revenue of an order, tax included: the 'TO' class of orders_total, the grand total actually paid - shipping included, unlike revenue_ht. Choose it ONLY when the question says tax included (TTC, taxes included, gross, what the customer paid).
text_metric_revenue_ht = MERCHANDISE revenue of an order: the 'TO' class minus the 'TX' class minus the 'SH' class - excluding tax AND excluding shipping. This is the DEFAULT basis: a bare revenue or turnover question takes this one, never revenue_ttc. Shipping is a service billed to the customer, not merchandise sold: it belongs to revenue_ttc, never here.
text_metric_line_revenue = Revenue of one product line, taken from orders_products, never from orders_total.
text_metric_average_cart = Average of the 'TO' class over orders, never of the 'ST' class.
text_metric_quantity_sold = Number of units sold, aggregated over orders_products.
text_metric_orders_count = Number of distinct orders.
text_metric_delivered_orders = Number of orders whose status means delivered, per the status map.
text_metric_cancelled_orders = Number of orders whose status means cancelled, per the status map. A cancellation is not a refund: never count the two together.
text_metric_refunded_orders = Number of orders whose status means refunded, per the status map. If no status of this shop carries that meaning, the question cannot be answered: say so, never fall back on cancellations.
text_metric_gross_margin_amount = Gross margin in currency: revenue of the sold lines minus their cost basis, where the cost basis is products_cost PLUS products_handling. Revenue alone, without the cost subtracted, is NOT a margin.
text_metric_gross_margin_percent = Gross margin as a RATE: gross_margin_amount divided by the revenue of the same sold lines, with the same cost basis (products_cost plus products_handling). Its variation is expressed in PERCENTAGE POINTS, never in percent.
text_metric_avg_shipping_delay = Average delay between the order date and its shipping date.
text_metric_discount_amount = Discount granted on orders: the 'CD' and 'DC' classes of orders_total, which the platform marks as deduction lines. Report it as its own figure: a discount is neither a revenue nor a margin, and it is never combined with one in a single arithmetic - do not subtract it from them and do not add it back.
text_metric_shipping_billed = Shipping BILLED to the customer: the 'SH' class of orders_total. It is what the shop charged for shipping, never what shipping cost the shop, and it is distinct from products_handling, which already sits inside the margin cost basis.
